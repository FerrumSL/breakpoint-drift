# NARMS cecal isolates: NARMS's 2023 relabelling

2022 isolates read with CLSI 2022; 2023 isolates with CLSI 2023. Changes are in percentage points of %R.

Up to 2022 NARMS reported cecal isolates with its own rules, e.g. a Salmonella ciprofloxacin MIC of 0.12 or more as R; from 2023 it follows CLSI, but keeps the gentamicin breakpoint CLSI cut in 2023. **Observed** is %R as NARMS reported it; **real** reads the MICs with CLSI 2022 and CLSI 2023.

- **Observed**: %R as originally reported in each period.
- **Real**: change in the values, with the guideline held fixed (mean of holding either one).
- **Artefact**: the rest of the observed change, caused by the breakpoint revision alone.
- **Verdict**: Fisher's exact test, Benjamini-Hochberg corrected across 24 pairs.
- **Read**: 2022 isolates that the old / new guideline could classify, of those tested. A capped MIC such as <=1 cannot be read against a breakpoint below it.

Verdicts: significant change 5, breakpoint artefact 2, noise 15, not interpretable 2.
