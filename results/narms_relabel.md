# NARMS cecal isolates: NARMS's 2023 relabelling

2022 isolates read with CLSI 2022; 2023 isolates with CLSI 2023. Changes are in percentage points of %R.

Up to 2022 NARMS reported cecal isolates with its own rules, e.g. a Salmonella ciprofloxacin MIC of 0.12 or more as R; from 2023 it follows CLSI, but keeps the gentamicin breakpoint CLSI cut in 2023. **Observed** is %R as NARMS reported it; **period** reads the MICs with CLSI 2022 and CLSI 2023.

- **Observed**: %R as originally reported in each period.
- **Period**: change in %R with the guideline held fixed (mean of holding either one). Not a causal estimate: sampling, panels and case mix can change too.
- **Guideline**: the rest of the observed change: the guideline revision, the lab's own reporting rules, and any disagreement between the lab's reading and this one.
- **Verdict**: which component is shown by its bootstrap test, Benjamini-Hochberg corrected across the 22 interpretable pairs; **Larger**: which component is larger, where that is shown. Fisher's exact test of the observed change decides *unresolved*.
- **Read**: 2022 isolates that the old / new guideline could classify, of those tested. A capped MIC such as <=1 is left out only if it can fall on both sides of the breakpoint.

Verdicts: period change 6, guideline change 2, both 0, unresolved 0, noise 14, not interpretable 2.
